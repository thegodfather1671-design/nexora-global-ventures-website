Building connections across markets.

NEXORA Global Ventures is a diversified commercial company established with a vision of connecting people, businesses, products, and opportunities across different sectors of the global economy.

We focus on commercial opportunities in gold ownership and related services, agricultural trading, commodity-related activities, business development, commercial trading, and company share participation.

Explore our four programmes.

01

Gold Bar Ownership

Opportunity to acquire eligible 99.9% pure gold at proposed discounted pricing, subject to market conditions, availability and the applicable written agreement.

  • Proposed 25%–30% discount
  • Flexible installment options
  • Gold bar sizes subject to availability
02

Gold Marketing & Sales

A sales and marketing programme providing eligible customers with assistance in marketing and reselling eligible gold.

  • Resale assistance
  • Marketing support
  • Applicable agency/service fee explained before proceeding
03

Pay & Earn

A commercial programme connecting qualifying deposits with selected agricultural trading and related business activities.

  • $200–$500: 3%
  • Above $500–$1,000: 5%
  • Above $1,000–$3,000: 10%
04

Limited Company Shares

A limited shareholder opportunity for up to 100 qualified participants, subject to the company's formal share structure and legal requirements.

  • 0.10% entry
  • 0.25% standard
  • 0.50% expanded
  • 1.00% maximum individual allocation
