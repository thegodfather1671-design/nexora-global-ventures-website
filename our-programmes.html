Our 4 NEXORA PROGRAMMES

NEXORA GLOBAL VENTURES offers four structured programmes, each designed around a different area of commercial opportunity. Participants should review the applicable terms, eligibility requirements, and programme conditions before participating.

1. Gold Bar Ownership Programme

A programme focused on the purchase and ownership of 99.9% pure gold bars. Available gold sizes, pricing, payment arrangements, delivery terms, and eligibility are subject to the applicable programme terms and market conditions.

2. Gold Marketing & Sales Programme

A commercial programme focused on marketing and selling gold products. Participants can engage in gold sales activities under the applicable programme structure, including relevant sales and agency arrangements.

3. Pay & Earn Programme

A programme connected to agricultural-related trading, farmers' markets, and food-security activities. Qualifying participation is subject to the applicable programme terms, eligibility requirements, and available opportunities.

4. Limited Company Shares Programme

A programme providing qualified participants with an opportunity to participate in limited company share allocations, subject to availability, eligibility, shareholder arrangements, and the applicable company terms.

Important: All NEXORA programmes are subject to individual terms, eligibility, availability, agreements, and applicable laws. Market-based activities, commodities, agricultural trading, and company shares involve risks, and outcomes are not guaranteed. Please contact a NEXORA representative for a clear explanation of any programme before participating.
